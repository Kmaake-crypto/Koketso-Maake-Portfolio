Koketso Maake

I am Koketso Maake, a Full-Stack Web Developer and professional Graphic Designer.

I design and build digital experiences that are visually striking and functionally powerful. With a strong foundation in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, I create responsive, user-focused websites while crafting bold, memorable graphic designs that communicate brand identity with clarity and impact.

I don't just write code, and I don't just design layouts. I treat the web as a canvas for premium user experiences. My journey started in visual branding, logo design, and digital layouts. But I quickly realized that a beautiful design is only half the battle it needs to live, breathe, and function flawlessly on the screen. That's what drove me into full-stack web development.
